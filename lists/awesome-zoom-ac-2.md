## Official

- [AC-2 Acoustic Pedal](https://zoomcorp.com/en/us/multi-effects/acoustic/ac-2/) — Official product page for the AC-2, an acoustic DI/preamp pedal with 16 source-instrument presets, 3-band EQ, anti-feedback, boost, tuner, and reverb.
- [AC-2 Support](https://zoomcorp.com/en/us/multi-effects/acoustic/ac-2/ac-2-support/) — Official support and downloads page with the operation manual, supplementary manual, and firmware updaters.
- [AC-2 Operation Manual (PDF)](https://zoomcorp.com/media/documents/E_AC-2.pdf) — Full operation manual (3 MB) covering connections, presets, EQ, reverb, boost, anti-feedback, and the tuner.
- [AC-2/AC-3 Supplementary Manual v1.1 (PDF)](https://zoomcorp.com/media/documents/E_AC-2_AC-3_v1.1_2.pdf) — Shared supplementary manual (369 KB) documenting changes and additions introduced after firmware v1.1 for AC-2 and AC-3.
- [AC-2 Windows firmware v1.10](https://zoomcorp.com/documents/116/AC-2_Win_v1.10_E.zip) — Official Windows firmware updater package, confirmed as a working ZIP containing the "ZOOM AC-2 System v1.10 Updater" executable.
- [AC-2 Mac firmware v1.10](https://zoomcorp.com/documents/117/AC-2_Mac_v1.10_E_2.zip) — Official macOS firmware updater package, version 1.10.

- [Reviewed: Zoom AC-2 Acoustic Creator — Mixdown Magazine](https://mixdownmag.com.au/reviews/guitar/reviewed-zoom-ac-2-acoustic-creator/) — Hands-on review; the writer was initially skeptical of acoustic modeling but found the AC-2 sounds natural, praising the tuner, reverb, and boost function.
- [Zoom's AC-2 pedal promises to restore guitar tone lost through acoustic pickups — MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/news/zooms-ac-2-pedal-promises-to-restore-guitar-tone-lost-through-acoustic-pickups) — Announcement coverage summarizing the 16 body presets, 3-band EQ, anti-feedback control, and up to 9 dB of boost gain at the $199.99 launch price.
- [New Zoom AC-2 Acoustic Creator Pedal — The Acoustic Guitar Forum](https://www.acousticguitarforum.com/forums/showthread.php?t=470400) — Long-running launch-era thread where forum members discuss first impressions; Zoom is noted as having made the AC-2 simpler than the earlier A3.
- [Zoom AC-2 Acoustic Creator users? — The Acoustic Guitar Forum](https://www.acousticguitarforum.com/forums/showthread.php?t=530186) — Owner thread with real-world impressions, including a report that the 10MΩ input impedance wasn't an ideal match for a K&K pickup but still sounded good.
- [Zoom AC-2, what are your thoughts??? — The Acoustic Guitar Forum](https://www.acousticguitarforum.com/forums/showthread.php?t=545122) — Further community discussion of ownership experiences with the pedal.
- [Zoom AC-2 with EUB? — TalkBass](https://www.talkbass.com/threads/zoom-ac-2-with-eub.1286441/) — Double-bass players discuss using the Dreadnought and Upright Bass presets with an electric upright bass and a high-pass filter; one user flags short battery life, including drain while the unit is off and unplugged.
- [Anyone still using zoom A3, AC2 or? — TalkBass](https://www.talkbass.com/threads/anyone-still-using-zoom-a3-ac2-or.1492074/) — Double-bass forum thread comparing long-term experience with the AC-2 against the older A3 and other DI/preamp options.
- [AC-2 Acoustic Creator — Audiofanzine (French)](https://fr.audiofanzine.com/preampli-guitare-a-modelisation/zoom/ac-2/) — Product/review database page (4.0/5 user rating) with a review describing use for gigs and rehearsals without an amp: mute/tuner mode, adjustable solo boost, feedback prevention, and an occasionally useful reverb.

## Notes

- The AC-2 has no dedicated editor software, companion app, or USB audio/MIDI interface mode — the only official PC/Mac downloads are the firmware updaters, and all presets and settings are edited directly on the pedal.
- The supplementary manual (v1.1) is shared between the AC-2 and its sibling, the AC-3; make sure you're reading the sections that apply to the AC-2.
- There is no dedicated tone/preset-sharing community for the AC-2 (unlike deeper modelers such as the HeadRush or Valeton GP-150) — its "presets" are the 16 built-in source-instrument types, not user-shareable patches.
- Power behavior varies by source: forum reports mention faster-than-expected battery drain on 2×AA power, so USB or AC-adapter power is worth considering for regular use.
- Always check firmware version compatibility before updating, and verify pickup type (piezo vs. magnetic) and input impedance suit your instrument before relying on the AC-2 live.
