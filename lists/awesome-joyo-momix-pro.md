## Notes

- The Momix Pro is a portable USB audio mixer/interface (two combo XLR/6.35mm channels, 48V phantom power, dual OTG smartphone ports, a basic 2-channel reverb, and a rechargeable battery) rather than a guitar multi-effects or amp-modeling processor; it has no onboard amp models, IR loader, or savable effects presets.
- JOYO does not publish dedicated desktop or mobile editor software for the Momix Pro. It is designed to be used alongside third-party recording, DAW, or live-streaming apps (e.g. GarageBand) on a connected phone or computer, rather than programmed through its own app.
- No public firmware-update procedure, preset-sharing community, or dedicated forum/subreddit was found for this specific model at the time of writing.
- JOYO also sells the separate, similarly named "Momix" (original) and "Momix Cab" products; their manuals and pages are easy to confuse with the Momix Pro's — double-check the model name before downloading.
