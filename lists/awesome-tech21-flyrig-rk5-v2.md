## Official

- [RK5 v2 product page (archive)](https://www.tech21nyc.com/archive/rk5-2/) — Official Tech 21 product page for the RK5 v2, covering the boost/compression, OMG Overdrive with fuzz switch, SansAmp section, reverb, delay, roto (rotary speaker), tuner, headphone output, and XLR DI, plus dimensions, weight, and included power supply.

- [RK5 v3 product page](https://www.tech21nyc.com/products/flyrig/rk5v3/) — Successor to the v2 (announced December 2024): adds a switchable pre/post boost, an effects loop, and phantom power for the RK Killer Wail wah ([Premier Guitar coverage](https://www.premierguitar.com/tech-21-richie-kotzen-rk5)).
- [Tech 21 Videos](https://www.tech21nyc.com/tech-21-videos/) — Official video hub listing Tech 21's own RK5 v2 demos, including versions by Brett Kingman and Vince Genella, and appearances with Richie Kotzen at the Iridium (NYC) and NAMM 2020.

## Notes

- The RK5 v2 is a fully analog pedal with no firmware, editor software, MIDI presets, or digital patches — there is nothing to update or back up, and any "editor" or "firmware" resource claiming otherwise is not for this device.
- Tech 21 discontinued the RK5 v2 in favor of the RK5 v3 (announced December 2024), which swaps the v2's FET compressor for a switchable pre/post boost, adds an effects loop, and adds phantom power for the RK Killer Wail wah — check listings carefully on the used market, since sellers sometimes blur the two versions.
- Some older Tech 21 review PDFs and forum threads (e.g. reviews dated 2014–2016) cover the original RK5, not the v2; confirm the version before relying on settings advice from an older write-up.
- The manual's general tone-stack tip — since most tube-amp tone stacks cut mids when every knob is maxed, start with bass and treble low and mid high on the SansAmp EQ, then adjust to taste — is a reasonable starting point for dialing in the SansAmp section.
