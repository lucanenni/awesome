# Awesome Donner MEDO

> A curated list of official and community resources for the Donner MEDO, a portable battery-powered sampling groovebox and Bluetooth MIDI controller.

## Contents

- [Official](#official)
- [Manuals](#manuals)
- [Community and Reviews](#community-and-reviews)

## Official

- [Donner downloads (UK)](https://uk.donnermusic.com/pages/download) — Official download page listing the MEDO manual and the MEDO Synth desktop app for Windows and Mac (v0.2.1 at the time of checking); regional pages such as [Australia](https://au.donnermusic.com/pages/download) (MEDO SYNTH v0.2.2) and the [Canada manuals page](https://ca.donnermusic.com/pages/manuals) can list different versions.
- [Donner Play on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/donner-play/id1632150488) and [on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.donnermusic.doriff&hl=en_US) — Official companion app for playing and for firmware updates (connect the device with the supplied data cable, check the version in settings, and upgrade).
- [Donner MEDO on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Donner-MEDO-Bluetooth-Controller-Groovebox/dp/B0D4QP4QMM) — Retail listing with the spec summary; it names Donner Play and MEDO SYNTH as the supported software and a one-year warranty.

## Manuals

- [Donner MEDO manual (ManualsLib)](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/3611088/Donner-Medo.html) — Mirror of the portable synthesizer manual, including the firmware update steps.
- [Donner MEDO user manual (Manuals+)](https://manuals.plus/donner/medo-portable-bluetooth-midi-controller-manual) — Readable manual mirror.

## Community and Reviews

- [Donner Intros MEDO Portable Groovebox (Synthtopia)](https://www.synthtopia.com/content/2023/10/08/donner-intros-medo-portable-groovebox/) — Launch announcement: drum, chord, and bass tracks, looping, and a built-in sampler.
- [Donner MEDO on Kickstarter (Synth Anatomy)](https://synthanatomy.com/2024/08/donner-medo-new-portable-battery-powered-sampling-groovebox-now-on-kickstarter.html) — Coverage of the crowdfunding launch.
- [Donner MEDO Review: A Budget-Friendly Sampler That Shines Outside the Studio (Magnetic Magazine)](https://magneticmag.com/2024/08/donner-medo-review/) — Review praising the hardware and noting weaker software: no custom drum sounds or sample upload at the time.
- [Donner MEDO review: a well-thought out groovebox that has yet to unlock its full potential (Floyd Steinberg, YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NHzXRHZu1DY) — Video review.

## Notes

- No custom firmware, reverse-engineering project, or third-party editor for the MEDO was found on GitHub or in the [altstrument](https://altstrument.org/) directory when this list was written; the only software is Donner's own.
- App-store reviewers of Donner Play criticize missing editing features (piano roll editing, custom drum kits, MIDI editing, mixer), so check the current app before relying on a workflow.
- Firmware updates go through Donner Play; back up any projects before updating.
