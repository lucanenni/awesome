## Software and Firmware

- [Downloads](https://www.meloftyaudio.com/support/downloads) — Official downloads page. At the time of writing (all dated 27 October 2026) it lists Axom Controller V1.9.2 for Windows and macOS, the MELOFTY App V1.9.2 and the Axom Max V1.9.2 firmware (1.20 GB), plus the MELOFTY USB Audio Driver V6.0.0 (13 October 2026). The download buttons are served from the site's own API, so use this page rather than a direct link.

## Community and Reviews

- [Bonedo: Test Melofty Axom Max](https://www.bonedo.de/artikel/test-melofty-axom-max-konkurrenz-fuer-line-6-neural-kemper-und-co/) — German hands-on test (4.5/5) covering the 7-inch touch display, nine footswitches, expression pedal, 400+ amp models, NAM A2 and IR loading, synth engine and tone capture.
- [Bonedo: Guitar Summit 2026 first look](https://www.bonedo.de/artikel/guitar-summit-2026-melofty-axom-max-audio-processor-die-neue-modeling-generation/) — German trade-show preview of the amp modeling, multi-effects, synth, amp capturing, AI tone assistant and audio interface.
