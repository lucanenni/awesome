## Resources

### LIVTRA downloads

[https://livtramusic.com/downloads](https://livtramusic.com/downloads)

Official firmware, ToneCommand software, manuals, and release notes for NANOCORE and other LIVTRA devices.

### LIVTRA NANOCORE product page

[https://livtramusic.com/products/nanocore/content-en.html](https://livtramusic.com/products/nanocore/content-en.html)

Official product information covering effects, RESONA-ready captures, imports, software, and custom themes.

### LIVTRA Music

[https://livtramusic.com/](https://livtramusic.com/)

Official LIVTRA site with product information for NANOCORE, MOSAIC, VELRIX, and related devices.

### ToneCommand for Android

[https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.livtra.nanocore](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.livtra.nanocore)

Android companion app for connecting to NANOCORE, editing presets, managing blocks, and importing/exporting bundles.

## Notes

- Always back up presets before updating firmware or importing third-party files.
- Verify exact model and firmware compatibility before using a preset or software package.
- Unofficial mirrors and community resources should be checked against the manufacturer’s documentation.
