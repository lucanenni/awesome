# Awesome Music Gear

[![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A collection of curated lists ("awesome lists") of official and community resources for guitar multi-effects processors, amp modelers, synthesizers, and related music gear.

Each device has its own list with official downloads/manuals, unofficial editors and software, preset packs, and community documentation. See the [Contributing](#contributing) section if you'd like to add a resource or a new device list.

## Contents

### Multi-Effects & Amp Modelers

- [HeadRush](lists/awesome-headrush.md) — Prime, Core, and Flex Prime
- [LIVTRA](lists/awesome-livtra.md) — NANOCORE and MOSAIC pocket NAM/IR pedals, NEXFOOT MIDI controller, SKYTUNER tuner
- [MOOER Prime M2](lists/awesome-mooer-prime-m2.md)
- [Roger Linn AdrenaLinn III](lists/awesome-roger-linn-adrenalinn-iii.md) — Beat-synced guitar effects processor with amp modeling and a built-in drum machine
- [Sonicake Pocket Master](lists/awesome-sonicake-pocket-master.md) — Pocket-size amp/effects modeler with NAM and IR support
- [Tech 21 Fly Rig RK5 v2](lists/awesome-tech21-flyrig-rk5-v2.md) — Richie Kotzen signature all-analog pedalboard-in-a-box
- [Valeton GP-150](lists/awesome-valeton-gp-150.md)

### Synthesizers & MIDI Controllers

- [M-VAVE FM-1](lists/awesome-mvave-fm-1.md) — Pocket FM synthesizer
- [M-VAVE SMK-37 Elite](lists/awesome-mvave-smk-37-elite.md) — MIDI keyboard controller with built-in DX7-style FM synth engine
- [Donner MEDO](lists/awesome-donner-medo.md) — Portable sampling groovebox and Bluetooth MIDI controller
- [M-VAVE Chocolate](lists/awesome-mvave-chocolate.md) — Chocolate and Chocolate Plus wireless MIDI foot controllers

### Guitars

- [Line 6 Variax 500](lists/awesome-line6-variax-500.md) — First-generation modeling guitar and its legacy Workbench software

### Acoustic Pedals & DI Boxes

- [M-VAVE Cube Baby AC](lists/awesome-mvave-cube-baby-ac.md) — Acoustic guitar multi-effects pedal with IR cabinet sims
- [Zoom AC-2](lists/awesome-zoom-ac-2.md) — Acoustic Creator DI/preamp pedal

### Practice Amps & Speakers

- [JBL BandBox](lists/awesome-jbl-bandbox.md) — BandBox Solo and Trio portable guitar amps with Stem AI

### Mixers & Audio Interfaces

- [Joyo Momix Pro](lists/awesome-joyo-momix-pro.md) — Portable 2-channel USB audio mixer/interface

## Related Directories

- [altstrument](https://altstrument.org/) — Third-party directory of alternative firmware and tools for music hardware (about 900 firmwares across 200+ devices); it has dedicated pages for the M-VAVE [FM-1](https://altstrument.org/devices/m-vave-fm-1), [SMK-37](https://altstrument.org/devices/m-vave-smk-37), and [Chocolate](https://altstrument.org/devices/m-vave-chocolate).
- [awesome-music-gear-firmware](https://github.com/Lapin/awesome-music-gear-firmware) — Another curated, links-only list of custom firmware for music gear (M-VAVE FM-1 and SMK-37 ports, Zoom MultiStomp firmware, and more).

## Contributing

Contributions are welcome! Each list has its own `Contributing` section with guidance on what to include (tested device, firmware version, operating system, and a short description of the resource). To add a new device, create a new `lists/awesome-<device>.md` file following the structure of the existing lists and add it to the Contents above.
